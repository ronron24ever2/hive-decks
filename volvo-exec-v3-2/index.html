The physical AI company for industrial machinery.

Hive is building a general-purpose AI model that learns to operate any industrial machine. Not automation scripts per task. Not remote control. A single intelligence layer trained across machine types, sites and conditions, enabling all industrial machines to operate with supervised physical AI.

01

One model, every machine

A generic AI model supporting the whole machinery fleet.

Not a separate integration per machine type. Hive trains one model that generalizes across wheel loaders, haulers, excavators and forklifts. The same intelligence layer adapts to new tasks and new iron without starting from scratch.

02

Fully packaged service

Ops center, trained operators, 24/7 coverage as one service.

Hive is not software you install. It is a packaged service. Operations center in Kristiansand, trained remote operators, redundant connectivity, 24/7 coverage. Nothing Volvo needs to replicate internally.

03

Human-in-the-loop by design

Operators in the loop, not stripped out of the system.

When the AI hesitates, a trained remote operator takes over instantly. Supervised physical AI is the product, not a fallback. Customers never see an unplanned stop.

04

Retrofit, not replacement

Runs on machines your customers already own.

Volvo L60 to L350 wheel loaders (MY 2018+) supported today, phasing to full Volvo CE portfolio. Dealer-installed kit. Fleet goes from standard to supervised physical AI in days, not quarters.

THE COMMERCIAL MODEL

A preferred partnership built on recurring revenue, shared data, and joint market expansion. Running through the dealer network you already have.

PILLAR 01

Shared recurring revenue

20% of every Hive physical AI subscription on any Volvo-sold machine, not just new sales. Paid continuously. A new recurring revenue line for Volvo CE on new machines, and for dealers on retrofit of the existing installed base.

PILLAR 02

Operational data sharing

Raw operational telemetry from every Hive-equipped Volvo-sold machine, flowing to Volvo R&D, product development, and service optimization. Unmatched real-world fleet visibility, governed by clear data agreements.

Increased market share

Physical AI becomes the reason customers choose Volvo over Cat, Komatsu, or any mid-segment alternative. Deployed through the local dealership, with Hive doing the commercial work alongside your team.
