THE FORCING FUNCTION

Manual labour is constrained.

Automation is no longer optional — the alternative, hiring more operators, is not viable.
Three constraints, all structural, all getting worse. Let's use logistics as an example:

SUPPLY

30–50%

Annual operator turnover in EU logistics. Workforce shrinking, aging out faster than replaced. Every rotation re-opens a productivity gap.

COST

~70%

Of a 2-shift forklift's loaded TCO is the operator. Wages up 4–7% per year. Shift premiums, sickness cover and onboarding outpace machine cost.

RELIABILITY

5×

Forklift-operator injury rate vs. the average warehouse role. Shifts go uncovered. Safety incidents block throughput. Iron sits idle 34% of the time.

The labour you can hire isn't enough. The labour you can't hire is the bottleneck.
