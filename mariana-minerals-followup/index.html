04 · VENDOR SCORECARD
EVALUATION CRITERIA

Six questions to ask every wheel-loader vendor.

We've been on the buyer's side of an autonomy evaluation, and these are the questions that separated vendors who could actually run a site from vendors who could run a demo. Ask us first, then send them to the others.

01
Productive-uptime guarantee in the contract, with financial penalty if missed?
Look for a specific percentage tied to scheduled production hours, with a defined remedy if it's missed. Anything softer than that is marketing.
02
When the AI becomes uncertain mid-cycle, what happens in the next five seconds, and who's accountable for the downtime?
The right answer involves a live operator who can pick the cycle up without breaking it. If the machine stops and waits for a call-out, you've bought a stoppage system rather than a production system.
03
Where is the 24/7 supervised ops center, and what's the operator-to-machine ratio today?
A real address, actual shift coverage, and a ratio backed by telemetry. A 24/7 ops center is a P&L line that most VC-backed vendors can't justify carrying.
04
Productivity from week one, before site mapping and model training are complete?
A real number, backed by a remote-operator fallback that runs from day one. Vendors who need three to six months of mapping before production are selling you a pilot.
05
Coexistence with your existing haul autonomy, under your orchestration layer?
A clean yes, plus a defined integration contract with your fleet management system. Look for a vendor who plugs under your mine plan and dispatch via a high-level command API, owns the per-machine work, and leaves orchestration to you. Vendors who want to own the dispatch layer will eventually force you to rip out your haul stack.
06
If acquired in the next twelve months, what's the change-of-control commitment to existing customers?
Look for a contractual assignment clause that locks your roadmap and SLA terms through any change of control, plus a source-code escrow option on the safety-critical components. Independent ownership today is a plus, given how much consolidation has happened in this space recently.
Send these to us first, and we'll give you written answers before the next call. PARTNERSHIP, NOT PITCH
