02 · MISSION
We sell productive machine-hours, not software
Mission

We sell productive machine-hours, not software.

Hive retrofits industrial machines and runs them as a service. A supervising operator at our centre handles the edge cases; the model runs the routine. Customers buy the work, not the platform.

The field is the only judge. A demo or a dashboard can be wrong. A machine running an eight-hour shift in a quarry, in winter, without a human touching it, cannot.

05 · WHAT WE DO
Two parts. One service.
What we do

Two parts. One service.

VISION · LANGUAGE · ACTION
The model

Cameras and lidar on the machine. A model trained on labelled cycles decides what to do next. An action expert turns that decision into joint commands at the machine layer.

The architecture works across forklifts, wheel loaders, terminal tractors and cranes. The customer keeps the machine, the dealer and the service contract; we add the autonomy on top.

KRISTIANSAND
The ops centre

A supervising operator takes over the moment the model loses confidence. The handover happens in under five seconds. Every intervention is two things at once: a guarantee that the work gets done, and a training signal that makes the next machine better.

Operators are not a scaffold on the way to "full autonomy." They are the engine that makes autonomy possible.

It's not programming. It's not automation. It is proper AI, supervised by people who know the field.

06 · HOW WE GOT HERE
From offshore cranes to wheel loaders
How we got here

From offshore cranes to wheel loaders.

2009Red Rock founded
2021Ocean Infinity buys Red Rock
2022Hive Autonomy incorporated

In 2009 Christoffer Jørgenvåg founded Red Rock in Kristiansand to automate offshore lifting — the heave-compensation hardware and remote-supervision software that lets a crane move heavy loads between a ship rolling on the sea and a platform fixed above. Over twelve years the team shipped more than three hundred mission-critical systems into the offshore industry.

In October 2021, Ocean Infinity — the Austin-based autonomous-maritime operator behind the twelve-vessel Armada fleet — bought Red Rock and folded the remote-supervision stack into its autonomous ocean operations.

The same engineering team kept building. Worked with several Norwegian industrial players on terminal tractors, gantry cranes and intralogistics. Self-funded most of the way.

Hive Autonomy was incorporated in August 2022. Forty people today, growing.

Red Rock 3D motion-compensated crane — offshore wind, North Sea.

11 · VISION
Where we're building toward
Where we're going

What LLMs do for software, Physical AI does for the real world.

Imagine building in the real world the way kids build in Minecraft. You want a house in a certain style — you place the walls, walk through it, change your mind, take a wing down, try a different roofline. The machine does the building. The cost is energy.

That is the shift LLMs created for software. You sit with ChatGPT or Claude and you build something just to see what it looks like. You throw it away if it doesn't work. The friction is gone.

Physical AI brings the same shift to the rest of the world. Every machine on every site assignable to a task at the cost of energy. A hundred thousand times faster than today. Materials reused, because tearing down and rebuilding is no longer the bottleneck.

Wheel-loader autonomy at a copper mine is one step toward that.

"Hey ChatGPT, finish this building." — already a billboard.
We started with the cycle in front of us. We're building for the one ahead.
