EXTREME ENVIRONMENTS · FROZEN OPERATIONS

Engineered to keep
working at −20 °C.

Cold doesn't pause operations — it removes the people who run them. HIVE hardens every layer of the machine so autonomy holds where a human shift can't.

SENSORS & PERCEPTION

Heated lidars and sensors are Industrial grade IP 67-69 to beat the fogging and condensation of freezer transitions, and are specified to −40 °C.

COMPUTE & ELECTRONICS

Temperature controlled enclosures keep compute in range with a fully encapsulated cabinet.

NAVIGATION & SAFETY

Point-cloud tuning for reflective floors; localisation and braking models account for ice, slip and longer stops.

THE TRANSITION PROBLEM

The hardest problem isn't the cold — it's the boundary. Every pass between ambient and −20 °C drives condensation, thermal shock and fog. Hive has been tested in the Transition zone to build up a robust operational Best Practice for the transition.
